Link Roundup Tool

Save quotes while you browse, then paste the roundup into Substack.

This page installs a bookmarklet, stores captured links in your browser, and exports a rich-text roundup with clickable titles, quotes, timestamps, and your notes.

Install

Open this page from a stable URL, then drag the button below into Chrome’s bookmarks bar.

Save Quote to Roundup
Building bookmarklet...
  1. Select a quote on any article page.
  2. Click the bookmarklet.
  3. Add your note in the modal and press Save.
  4. Come back here when you want to export the roundup.

For local-only use, serving this page at http://127.0.0.1:8000/ works well with a tiny local web server.

Saved Links

Entries are stored in this browser via localStorage. Export when you are ready to paste into Substack.
