# London Trip Itinerary

## Overview
This itinerary is for a **9-night London trip**, arriving **midday on Friday** and departing the following **Sunday**. It is designed at a **medium pace**, with **1–2 meaningful activities per day**, and emphasizes:

- British history and art
- World War II history
- Rock and popular music history
- Food and markets
- Walkable neighborhoods and manageable transit

---

## Day 1 — Friday: Arrival & Easy London Walk

  - Covent Garden
  - Leicester Square
  - Trafalgar Square

### Evening
- Dinner options:
  - Dishoom

## Notes
- Keep museum visits to roughly **90–120 minutes** unless one is a major priority.
- Book **Churchill War Rooms** in advance.
- Keep one or two flexible slots open for weather, fatigue, or a spontaneous neighborhood detour.
- If literature becomes a bigger part of the trip, the best additions are:
  - Charles Dickens Museum
  - British Library Treasures Gallery
  - Shakespeare’s Globe
